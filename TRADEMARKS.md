# Trademark policy

The code is free software under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). **The name and the look are not.** The GPL covers
copyright, not trademarks, so this policy explains how the Project's brand may be used.

The Project's brand ("the Marks") is:
- its name and wake word ("Hey Mint", "Mint");
- its logo and app icon;
- the orb character: its face, expressions and the critters.

All belong to shivatmax (github.com/shivatmax).

## You may, without asking

- Say that your work is **based on**, **compatible with** or **a fork of** the Project, in plain text
  (e.g. "Assistant X, a fork of Hey Mint").
- Use the name to refer to the Project in articles, videos, talks, reviews, tutorials and teaching.
- Keep the name in unmodified copies of the source, the copyright notices and this file.

## You may not, without written permission

- Distribute a **modified** version, or a paid, hosted or bundled version, under the Project's name,
  logo, icon or orb character. Forks must use a different name and icon and must not suggest they are
  the official Project.
- Use the Marks in a product, company, domain, app store listing, social account or ad name, or in a way
  that suggests the Maintainer endorses you.
- Use a name or logo confusingly similar to the Marks for software or AI assistants.

## Why

People install an assistant that hears their microphone and uses their Mac. They need to know which
build is the real one. A clear name for each fork protects users, and the Project.

To ask for permission, open an issue or contact the Maintainer through
[github.com/shivatmax](https://github.com/shivatmax).
