# Code of conduct

Hey Mint follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome newcomers, assume good intent, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not
acceptable anywhere in the project's spaces.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/shivatmax/hey-mint/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@shivatmax](https://github.com/shivatmax). Reports are handled confidentially,
and maintainers may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
