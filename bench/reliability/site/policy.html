Returns and refunds

Northwind Outfitters wants you to be happy with every order.

Refund window

Unworn items can be returned for a full refund within 37 days of delivery. Sale items can be exchanged but not refunded.

Shipping costs

Return shipping is free for members; everyone else pays a flat fee of 6.50 EUR.

Contact

Write to returns@northwind.example with your order number.
