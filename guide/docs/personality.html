Personality and voices

Give Mint a personality in one line, see its expressions and tricks, and pick one of 30 voices.

Personality

Give Mint a personality in one line: funny, calm, argumentative, sarcastic, motivating, formal, or your own words. Settings ▸ General ▸ Personality, up to 100 characters, or pick a preset.

Asked “Is pineapple on pizza a good idea?”Mint answered
Default“That's a classic debate!…”
Argumentative“Absolutely not, Boss!…”
Funny“The ultimate food debate… a sweet and savory masterpiece.”

It changes only the tone. Mint's rules, tools, short replies and safety stay the same. A new personality applies in a few seconds, once Mint is quiet, and the conversation carries on.

Expressions and tricks

Fourteen expressions and eight tricks, each a real 60 fps animation. Mint uses them sparingly on its own, and whenever you ask.

  • do a loop the loop
  • a figure eight
  • chase a star
  • bounce like a ball
  • go a little up
  • go back
  • do all your tricks

Feelings from the moment. Mint reads the conversation as it goes (about half a second, on its own): a sad face when you share something sad, a laugh at your joke or its own, applause for your good news, hearts when it comforts you, a blush when you praise it - and nothing at all for ordinary requests. When it finishes a task for you, it slides on its sunglasses. “Expressions during conversation” in the menu turns this off.

Voices

Any of Gemini's 30 voices, in any speaking style you describe. It switches between turns and keeps the conversation.

  • use a deeper voice
  • sound more cheerful
  • talk slower
  • use Puck
