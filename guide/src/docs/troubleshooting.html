The log and your files

Where to look when something is off, and how to start over on one thing without losing the rest.

WhatWhere
The log~/Library/Logs/Mint/mint.log. Report a problem… copies its recent errors for you, with personal details removed.
Keys, settings, memory, skills~/Library/Application Support/Hey Mint for the downloaded app (~/Library/Application Support/Mint for an app built from source). Updating or reinstalling keeps them.
Some feature files~/Library/Application Support/Mint: the clipboard history, the Google Meet browser profile, backups of changed files.
What Mint makes~/Documents/Mint, unless you picked another folder in Settings ▸ Storage & Privacy.
  • Change a key: Settings ▸ Accounts & keys, or hold ⌥ while opening Hey Mint.
  • Check your setup: Settings ▸ Updates & Help ▸ Check my setup runs the same checklist as python -m mint --doctor from a source checkout: macOS, the Gemini key (checked with Google, shown only as ••••last4), the voice models, permissions, the app and updates, Claude Code's hooks, Telegram and email control, disk space. Each line says ✓, ! or ✗ and what to do.
  • See the welcome again: Welcome tour… in the menu bar.
  • Check a setting by hand: Settings ▸ Updates & Help ▸ The settings file ▸ Edit…; every setting and its default is on the Configuration page.
  • The app can't be opened: use the one-line install, which approves it with macOS for you.
