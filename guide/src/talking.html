Waking Mint and asking

Say “Hey Mint”, then ask in your own words. Your words appear in a caption beside the orb as you speak; Mint's reply is captioned the same way.

Stopping it

“Stop”, “cancel”, “never mind” or “hold on” halts everything at once, including clicks and keys already queued. The ■ button in the chat does the same.

Basics

The chat window

Press ⌘J (or click the orb) to open the chat. Everything said is there, every action with a ✓ or ✗, and a box to type in. Typing never turns the microphone on.

{{clip chat | The chat opening. The conversation, the list of actions, and the typing box.}}

The buttons along the top

ButtonWhat it does
👁 EyeShow or hide Mint in screen sharing (red when visible)
⋯ MoreSummarise and compact, clear the chat, start a new session
☾ MoonSend Mint to sleep
🎙 MicMicrophone on or off
🔊 SpeakerSpoken replies on or off
⚙ SettingsSettings, voice training, quitting
✕ CloseClose the chat (Esc works too)

Keeping it tidy

  • summarise and compact the session
  • clear the chat
  • start a new session

Compact writes a short summary and restarts Mint with just that as context. Clear empties the window but keeps memory. New session saves this conversation to memory and starts fresh.

Basics

Languages and names

Mint understands English and Hindi, and the two mixed. Names from your accounts, Slack channels and routines are given to the transcriber as vocabulary, so it spells them right. When it mishears, tell it once (see Words it gets wrong).
