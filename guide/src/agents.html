Agents

What an agent is

Mint (the orb) talks with you and routes work. When a job is big (multi-step research, a long document, building code or a website, anything that produces files) Mint hands it to an agent and keeps talking to you while it works.

{{clip agent-life | An agent's whole life. Astra pops out of the box, thinks (…), searches the web (magnifier), reads a page (globe), asks you a question (?), gets the answer (hearts), writes the brief (pencil), saves a file, finishes with a somersault, and hops back into the box.}}
  • Every agent has a name, a colour, a job, its own instructions, a set of tools, and a workspace folder in ~/Documents/Mint/agents/<name>.
  • Agents run on OpenAI GPT-6 Luna (through OpenRouter, or straight to OpenAI when that key is the one that works). Codex is OpenAI Codex itself.
  • Mint only delegates when a job truly needs it: quick answers and anything on your screen it does itself.

Giving an agent a job

Just ask for the result. Mint picks the right agent, or you can name one.

  • have Astra research the best mechanical keyboards and write a brief
  • ask Codex to make a tiny hello-world page
  • get Sage to write a two-page report on this, as a PDF
  • Luna and Astra, at the same time: Luna builds the gridworld, Astra finds three papers on reward shaping
  • give Astra what ChatGPT just wrote and have her check the sources
  1. Mint writes the brief. Full instructions (goal, constraints, what to deliver), how hard to think (none, low or medium), and why it needs an agent at all. Tasks under six words are refused as “do it yourself”.
  2. The critter pops out and the chat shows “Astra started: …”.
  3. It works in its own folder, with its own tools. You keep talking to Mint about anything else.
  4. When it finishes, Mint tells you the outcome, the chat shows it, and it goes into memory.
“Give Astra what ChatGPT just wrote” hands the agent the exact text of the window in front, links included, instead of Mint retyping it.

Agents working as a team

Mint gives a job to the agent best placed to lead. The lead brings in teammates itself when their specialty helps (Sage writing a report asks Astra for the research; Luna asks Codex to build something) and reports back for all of them.

{{clip agent-team | A helper's helper. Sage calls Astra in: Astra pops out of Sage, a little smaller, stands beside her, searches, and hops back into Sage when done.}}
  • ask Sage to write the report, and get the research from Astra first
  • have Luna build it, with Codex doing the web page
  • Sage, no helpers this time
RuleDetail
FoldersThe lead's folder is the mission root; each helper works in helpers/<name>/ and can read everything, so nobody overwrites anybody
Team boardAgents share findings on a board (board.md); teammates working right now see new notes at their next step
Limits2 levels below the lead, 6 agents at once, no asking an agent already up your own chain, a helper stops after 20 minutes; stopping the lead stops its helpers
Your sayMint can limit helpers (“only Astra”, or none)
MemoryEvery finished run is logged; a new run starts with the most relevant earlier results and what Mint knows about you

  • make Nova think harder
SettingMeaning
nameWhat you call it (and its critter's name tag)
roleOne line: what it is for (Mint uses this to pick agents)
instructionsIts own system prompt: how it should work
thinkingnone, low or medium (its usual effort)
webWhether it may search the web and read pages (on by default)
colourpink, purple, violet, teal, green, orange, amber, yellow, blue, red, cyan, mint, or a hex code

Agents are kept in agents.json; edit it by hand if you like. Everyone gets files, asking you, and progress reports; web tools unless you say no.
