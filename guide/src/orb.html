The orb

Worked, or didn't

{{clip done-fail | A ✓ with a hop when a click worked; a ✗ with a shake (and a tiny rain cloud) when typing failed. Then the face comes back.}}

The chat lists the same action with a green ✓ or a red ✗, so you can scroll back and see what happened.

The orb

Long tasks

{{clip progress | A three-step plan: “Step 1/3”, “Step 2/3”, “Step 3/3” in the caption, a progress ring round the orb, then confetti.}}

For anything with three or more steps Mint makes a plan first (plan_task), marks each step done as it goes, and celebrates at the end. The chat shows a progress bar too.

The orb

On the screen itself

Only two small things are drawn on your screen, both click-through: a ripple with a paw print where a click lands, and a thin outline round the box being typed into.
