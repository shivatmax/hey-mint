How it goes

Talk to it like a person.

No commands to learn. Mint works out how to do it with your Mac's own controls, and shows you each step.

  1. Say “Hey Mint”

    The wake word is heard on your Mac. Nothing leaves it until you say it, and only your voice counts.

  2. Ask your way

    Talk, or press ⌘J and type. Keep going without the wake word; it's one conversation.

  3. ✓

    Watch it happen

    The orb turns into what it's doing and shows a ✓. Say “stop” and everything halts.

How agents work
Safe by design

It asks before anything you can't undo.

Only your voice

Train it once and nobody else can wake it or talk over it.

No passwords

It never types a password or card number, and never saves one.

Asks first

Emails stay drafts. Messages and forms go out only when you said so.

Trash, not delete

“Delete” means the Trash, and files are backed up before Mint changes them.

Get Mint.

One app with everything inside. No Python, Homebrew or Terminal.
