Showing

Showing you where something is

Ask where something is, in any app: a PDF, a web page, a chat, a document, code. Mint finds the words on screen, scrolls to them if needed, marks them, and flies over beside the mark to look at it.

How it finds things

  1. The exact words, even when they wrap onto the next line; only those words are marked, not the whole paragraph.
  2. Most of the words, allowing for plurals and word endings (“refund” finds “refunds”).
  3. The meaning: Jev picks the line that says it from the real text on screen, or says none does.
  4. Not visible? It scrolls the window a screen at a time, down then up, and looks again.

A picture or chart with no text is marked from a screenshot (mark_area). Marks fade after about ten seconds, or say “clear the marks”. Name the app (“in the PDF”) and Mint brings it forward first.

Moving

Moving on command

Every move is a curved path at 60 frames a second: it speeds up, overshoots a touch and settles, leans into the turn, stretches when fast, and squashes like jelly when it lands. The new spot is kept.

  • go a little up
  • move left
  • go a little down
  • you're covering something
  • go back
  • go to the middle
  • circle around the window
SayWhat happens
“a little / more / a lot” up, down, left, rightAbout 70 / 160 / 320 points that way
“you're covering something”, “move away”Hops to the other end of the same edge
“go to the middle”The centre of the screen
“go back”Undoes the last move
“circle around the window”Once round the front window: slow into the corners, fast along the edges, then home
“move to the top left”A corner, kept as the orb's home
Moving

Tricks

Ask for one by name, or “do a trick” for a random one. Each ends back home with a little expression. “Do all your tricks” plays all eight back to back.

  • hops
  • do a loop the loop
  • a figure eight
  • bounce like a ball
  • zigzag like a bee
  • chase a star
  • peek
  • spin
  • do all your tricks

On its own

About once every ten minutes, only while you have left the computer alone for a few seconds and nothing is going on, it does a random trick. Turn that off with "wander": false in settings.json. Grab the orb and any move stops at once.
